STATEMENT OF WORK

This Statement of Work ("SOW") is entered into as of October 1, 2026, by and between Acme Widgets Inc. ("Client") and Freelance Consulting LLC ("Contractor").

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

Contractor shall assist with the development of a customer dashboard, as needed, including reasonable related work. Contractor shall provide deliverables per the schedule below:

Deliverable #1: Design mockups (wireframes + visual design)
Deliverable #2: Front-end implementation (React app, responsive)
Deliverable #3: Back-end API integration (Node.js, deployed alongside the dashboard)
Deliverable #4: User testing report

2. FEES AND PAYMENT

Total project fee: $48,000. Payment terms are net 60 days from invoice date. No deposit is required. Payment is subject to Client acceptance of the corresponding deliverable. A kill fee of $1,500 applies if Client terminates the engagement.

Each party shall hold the other party's Confidential Information in strict confidence in perpetuity. Contractor agrees that disclosure of any details of this engagement, including via portfolio or case study, constitutes a breach.

5. INDEMNIFICATION

Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client from any and all claims, damages, losses, and expenses (including attorneys' fees) arising from or related to this engagement, without limitation. Contractor's indemnification obligations shall not be subject to the liability cap set forth herein.

6. LIABILITY

Contractor's liability under this Agreement shall be unlimited with respect to claims arising from Contractor's breach of confidentiality or IP obligations. For all other claims, Contractor's aggregate liability shall be capped at $5,000.

7. TERM AND TERMINATION
